shot cheat sheet 🎬

camera moves for a travel montage. each frame loops the move; grid lines are the rule of thirds.

  • every place: wide → medium → close → detail → odd angle
  • hold 2 s still before and after each move
  • 24 fps, shutter 1/50 (2× frame rate)
  • slow-mo: shoot 60/120 fps, play at 24
  • lock AE/AF so exposure doesn't pump
  • cut on motion, roughly to the beat
  • vlog bits: 5–15 s, dropped between montage runs
  • Pocket 4 modes: Follow = everyday · Tilt Locked = flat horizon · FPV = rolls with your hand
  • joystick pans are smoother than turning your wrist
  • Pocket slow-mo: 4K/240 = 10× at 24 · D-Log only if you'll color-grade
